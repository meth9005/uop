The Foundation Course in English at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Peradeniya is a preparatory programme designed to equip students with the essential English language skills required for their undergraduate engineering studies. It provides a practical foundation in technical communication, academic reading, listening, speaking, and writing, with emphasis on using English effectively in an engineering context.

OUR MISSION

Empowering Engineers Through Language

Our mission is to equip engineering students with the English communication skills they need to thrive in a globally connected profession — from technical writing and oral presentation to creative and critical expression.

We believe that great engineers are also great communicators. This platform is a testament to the remarkable things our students achieve when language and creativity meet engineering curiosity.
